First launch

If macOS won’t open the app

Slovo is distributed without Apple notarization, so on first launch macOS may say it could not verify the app. This has nothing to do with your processor and takes a minute to resolve — either way works.

Via Privacy & Security
  1. Double-click the app and dismiss the warning with Done — not Move to Trash.
  2. Open System Settings → Privacy & Security.
  3. At the bottom of the Security section click Open Anyway and confirm.
Via Terminal

Remove the quarantine flag with a single command:

xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/Translator.app
